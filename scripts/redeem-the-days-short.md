# Spoken Text Test Short — Redeem the Days

Charles,

How God has blessed you! How you have received and recognized that blessing! I know one of your greatest fears that He would take it all away at some point. I was talking to God about you last night, and I realized God's wisdom and grace in his relationship with you throughout your years at Western. How significant that he blessed you so abundantly that you might see him as he is as a God of infinite grace and mercy. You see his forgiveness and acceptance in Christ. He wants you to know him as a God who wants to show you love even when you feel like you aren't worthy of it.

But I understand how difficult it has been for you to receive this love and blessing God has been pouring out on you. I remember experiencing some of your pain as you shared with me many of your experiences in junior high and high school. And now I see the truth of the scripture that says that his kindness that leads you to repentance. You've always had a fear of not being good enough, but you are already complete in Christ!

God has known this all along, and especially knows this is true in you, Charles. He's blessed you beyond what you deserve, or what you could ever merit or earn. Blessing upon blessing upon blessing. From housing to jobs to relationships, including Jeff and Mark. In every area of your life, he has brought these blessings. This is a debt you will NEVER be able to repay. And he doesn't even want you to try. Instead, He wants you to know grace in a new way. Unbounded. Limitless. Free. Without strings, no escape clause. To earn your trust, and for you to receive his love, acceptance, and forgiveness. I would not have said this to you a few years ago, but I confidently say it now.

Know that he loves you infinitely more than anyone else could or ever will, and delights to see you respond to this love.

What follows is a gift as well. I have personalized it because I think it's important for you to hear it said about yourself. I love you, Charles.

You are redeeming all the days of your life. YOU do not surrender to the lies the enemy throws at you. YOU have spent enough time in the past living for the lusts of the flesh, the lusts of the eyes, the pride of life. YOU do not live any longer solely for your own purposes and pleasures. YOU are not enslaved to your emotions. Daily YOU choose to allow God control your thoughts and give you his. He has a new purpose for you in Christ.

In Christ, YOU ARE a new creation. No matter how you feel or what you think. By Christ's blood YOU have been forgiven of all sin, and there are no longer, nor will there ever be, EVER, any condemning charges against you. You don't fear God's displeasure, nor do you fear falling into sin. You don't live to earn God's forgiveness, God has already forgiven you in Christ. You don't live today based on what happened yesterday or what might happen tomorrow. You don't live attempting to rewrite your past.

You redeem the days. You choose, as an act of your will, to be thankful in all circumstances. Daily, you give thanks for the man that God has made you, and, in Christ, who you have become and who you are becoming. You're a valuable and loved part of God's family. You're developing close friendships with others who love you in return. You rest secure in the place where God has placed you. You will rest secure in the security you have in Christ. You're finding your identity and security in the knowledge that you are a child of God. You're a saint set apart for God's purposes. You live in fellowship with Christ.

You redeem the days. You make a choice to be known by others, in all your weaknesses and strengths. You live in the light. You don't hide in the shadows. You allow the blood of Jesus Christ to continually cleanse you and set you free. You invite others to kneel at the cross with you in confession of Jesus as Savior and Lord. You are nurturing friendships that allow others freedom. Freedom to live without accusation or judgment from you or anyone else. You are learning to live without fear, since there is no condemnation in Christ Jesus. You're saying no to shame; it's being washed away by the blood of Christ.

In Christ, you are a new creation. You love others as Christ loves them, not based on gender, how old or young they are, where they came from or their status. You don't look at job titles, abilities, gifts or personalities. You have the mind of Christ. You recklessly and selflessly love and serve. You've already been loved by God's love in Christ, so you love others. You don't need to prove yourself as manly or strong, smart or witty, attractive or desirable. Christ has already accepted you unconditionally; nothing you do or say will make God love you more or less. You know this from the core of your being. Your relationship with Christ lets you keep loving others who aren't loving you in return. Because you are in Christ, you are free to give and receive real love regardless.

You are redeeming the days. You're growing every day in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ. You're able to put the needs of others before your own. You are truly alive.
